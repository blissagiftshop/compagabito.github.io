Our Story

A simple idea, made with heart.

The Gentle Shepherd Co. began with a simple idea: to create a plush keepsake that feels comforting, meaningful, and beautifully made.

We wanted to design something soft and huggable, but also heartfelt — a gentle reminder of faith, love, and comfort that can be treasured by children, families, and adults.

Every piece is thoughtfully designed, from the softness of the fabric to the little details that make it feel special. More than just a plush, our keepsakes are made to bring warmth to the heart, whether displayed at home, given as a gift, or carried along wherever life takes you.

At The Gentle Shepherd Co., we believe meaningful pieces should feel personal, comforting, and timeless.

What is The Gentle Shepherd Co.?

The Gentle Shepherd Co. creates premium faith-inspired keepsakes designed to bring comfort, warmth, and meaning into everyday life. Our pieces are thoughtfully made to be soft, huggable, giftable, and easy to treasure.

What makes your plush special?

Our plush is designed to feel comforting, cute, and meaningful without feeling overwhelming. We focus on soft materials, gentle details, and a warm design that can be loved by children, families, and adults.

Is the plush safe for children?

We aim to use soft, child-friendly materials, embroidered details, and thoughtful construction wherever possible. For young children, adult supervision is still recommended, especially when a design includes removable accessories or small decorative parts.

What materials are used?

Our plush is made with soft fabric and filled with PP cotton. Some designs may include embroidered details, removable accessories, fabric tags, or other small design elements depending on the product style.

How do I care for my plush?

We recommend gentle spot cleaning with a damp cloth and mild soap. Avoid machine washing, soaking, bleaching, or tumble drying to help preserve the shape, embroidery, and softness.

Do you accept bulk orders?

Yes. We welcome bulk-order inquiries for gifts, events, churches, schools, and special occasions. Please contact us for availability, pricing, and lead time.

Where do you ship from?

We ship from Fort BGC, Taguig, Philippines.

Do you ship nationwide?

Yes. We ship within the Philippines. Shipping options and rates may depend on your location and the courier available at the time of checkout.

Do you offer international shipping?

International shipping may be available upon request. Please contact us before placing an order so we can check available shipping options and estimated costs.

How long does delivery take?

Delivery time depends on your location and the courier. Once your order has been shipped, we will send tracking details when available.

What payment methods do you accept?

Available payment methods may include bank transfer, GCash, Maya, card payments, or other checkout options shown on the website at the time of purchase.

Can I change my order after placing it?

Please contact us as soon as possible. If your order has not yet been packed or shipped, we’ll do our best to help. Once an order has shipped, changes may no longer be possible.

Do you accept returns or exchanges?

Returns or exchanges are accepted for confirmed defects or quality concerns upon arrival. Please contact us within 24–48 hours of receiving your order and include your order number plus clear photos or videos for review.

Will the accessories be exactly the same?

Some accessories or small details may vary slightly depending on availability and production. We always aim to keep the overall look, quality, and design consistent.

Care Guide

Keep your keepsake soft and beautiful.

Gentle care helps preserve the plush’s shape, fabric, embroidery, and special details.

  1. 01
    Spot clean only

    Use a damp cloth and a small amount of mild soap.

  2. 02
    Do not soak

    Avoid machine washing or fully submerging the plush in water.

  3. 03
    Air dry naturally

    Do not bleach, tumble dry, iron, or expose to high heat.

  4. 04
    Handle accessories gently

    Remove or adjust accessories carefully to help preserve their shape and finish.
